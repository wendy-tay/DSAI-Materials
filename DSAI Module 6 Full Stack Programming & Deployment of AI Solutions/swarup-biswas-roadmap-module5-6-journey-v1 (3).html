The learning journey, end to end

Fifteen sessions take you from understanding what a language model is to shipping a deployed, multi-agent application. This page shows where each session sits in that journey and how the capstone project grows alongside it.

The capstone spine — one project, five deployments

Your capstone is not a separate assignment at the end; it is the same project growing through the whole journey. Each work sprint applies that day's material to your project, and five times along the way you deploy what you have.

Deployment 1

A full-stack, single-turn LLM app — your idea, live for the first time.

Deployment 2

A designed user experience on a deployed public website.

Deployment 3

Agentic RAG — your app now reasons over your own documents.

Deployment 4

Automated workflows with retrieval built in.

Deployment 5

Multi-agent orchestration — the finished capstone system.

How to use this roadmap

  • Before each session, find it on this page and read the arc it belongs to, so you know what the day is building towards.
  • Treat the four building blocks (sessions 6, 9, 10, and 12) as the load-bearing sessions; each one adds a capability your capstone depends on.
  • Come to every capstone work sprint with your project ready to extend — the sprints are where the course becomes your own work.
  • If you fall behind, prioritise the deployments; each one is a checkpoint that proves the previous arc landed.
