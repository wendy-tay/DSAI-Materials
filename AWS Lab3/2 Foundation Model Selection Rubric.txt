# Foundation Model Selection Rubric

## Categories and Criteria

### 1. **Alignment with Use Case Requirements**
- **Contextual Relevance**: Ability to generate content relevant to specific tasks (e.g., customer service, document summarization) (Max: 10 points)
  - **10**: Highly relevant content with minimal additional input
  - **7**: Mostly relevant content with some additional input
  - **5**: Relevant content with significant additional input
  - **3**: Somewhat relevant content with extensive additional input
  - **1**: Irrelevant content

- **Customization Flexibility**: Ability to fine-tune the model to specific internal processes and brand voice (Max: 10 points)
  - **10**: Highly customizable with minimal effort
  - **7**: Customizable with some effort
  - **5**: Moderately customizable with significant effort
  - **3**: Slightly customizable with extensive effort
  - **1**: Not customizable

### 2. **Performance and Scalability**
- **Initial Benchmark Performance**: Performance on standard benchmarks (Max: 10 points)
  - **10**: Excellent performance
  - **7**: Good performance
  - **5**: Average performance
  - **3**: Below average performance
  - **1**: Poor performance

- **Scalability**: Ability to handle large volumes of requests without significant performance degradation (Max: 10 points)
  - **10**: Excellent scalability
  - **7**: Good scalability
  - **5**: Moderate scalability
  - **3**: Poor scalability
  - **1**: Very poor scalability

### 3. **Ethical Considerations and Bias Mitigation**
- **Bias Detection and Mitigation**: Presence and effectiveness of tools for detecting and mitigating biases (Max: 10 points)
  - **10**: Comprehensive bias detection and mitigation tools
  - **7**: Effective bias detection tools with moderate mitigation
  - **5**: Basic bias detection tools with limited mitigation
  - **3**: Minimal bias detection tools with no mitigation
  - **1**: No bias detection or mitigation tools

- **Ethical Compliance**: Adherence to ethical guidelines and responsible AI principles (Max: 10 points)
  - **10**: Full compliance with ethical guidelines
  - **7**: Mostly compliant with ethical guidelines
  - **5**: Partially compliant with ethical guidelines
  - **3**: Minimally compliant with ethical guidelines
