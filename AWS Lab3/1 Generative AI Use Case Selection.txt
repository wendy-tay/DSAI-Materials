# Generative AI Use Case Selection Document

## Problem Statement
AnyOrganization faces challenges in customer engagement and employee productivity. The organization struggles to provide consistent, high-quality interactions and manage costs effectively. Employees spend significant time on repetitive tasks, reducing their capacity for strategic work. Generative AI presents a solution by enhancing customer service and automating routine tasks.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

- **Customer Interaction Enhancement**: Provide personalized, context-aware responses.
- **Employee Productivity Automation**: Automate routine tasks such as report generation.
- **Knowledge Management**: Create an AI-powered knowledge base for quick information retrieval.

### Non-Functional Requirements

- **Data Privacy and Security**: Ensure compliance with data protection regulations.
- **Bias Mitigation**: Detect and correct biases in AI-generated content.
- **Scalability and Performance**: Maintain high performance and scalability.

## Stakeholder Alignment

### Executive Engagement

- **Workshops and Presentations**: Educate executives on the value proposition.
- **Case Studies and Metrics**: Share successful case studies and KPIs.

### IT Collaboration

- **Early Involvement**: Involve IT early in planning and development.
- **Training and Upskilling**: Provide training programs for IT staff.

### End-User Feedback

- **Pilot Programs**: Launch pilot programs for end-user experience.
- **User Advisory Group**: Establish a group for ongoing feedback.

## Key Metrics

- **Customer Satisfaction**: Measure through surveys and feedback.
- **Response Time and Accuracy**: Track AI interaction performance.
- **Employee Productivity**: Measure time saved from automation.
- **Adoption Rate**: Track user adoption of AI solutions.
- **Cost Savings**: Calculate savings from reduced manual labor.

## Selection Criteria

- **Business Impact**: Potential to improve customer engagement and productivity.
- **Technical Feasibility**: Ability to integrate with existing systems and workflows.
- **Resource Availability**: Availability of necessary skills and budget.
- **Risk Assessment**: Evaluation of potential risks and mitigation strategies.

## Organizational Readiness

- **Training and Support**: Ensure availability of training and support resources.
- **Change Management**: Implement change management strategies to facilitate adoption.
- **Governance Framework**: Establish a framework for ethical AI use and security.

This document provides a structured approach for selecting generative AI use cases at AnyOrganization, ensuring alignment with business goals and stakeholder needs.
